## agricultural dark

Asset: `assets/agricultural-background-v4.png`

Base prompt:

Asset: a background-only portrait 3:4 graphic for a tyre ecommerce product card. The two attached images are STYLE references ONLY: retain their flat printed stencil / distressed ink approach and disciplined negative space, but DO NOT reuse their actual tread pattern or paint-swash composition. Create a NEW design depicting cultivated EARTH as flat rough-edged clod silhouettes and stippled dry-soil marks, with a clearly readable tractor HERRINGBONE tyre-track stencil. Matte graphite #20212d base. Muted deep-violet ink shapes (#40305f and #59407e), a few very small lime #d7f329 accent fragments. Most pixels must stay graphite or subdued violet. No neon glow or dramatic lighting. The material must read as GRAPHIC SCREENPRINT, with crisp imperfect cutout shapes, controlled grain and dry distressed edges. NO photographic soil, NO realistic stones, NO 3D, NO embossed relief, NO light beams, NO gradients that make rocks look volumetric, NO large geometric stripe banners, NO mountains, NO literal scene. One broad diagonal track crosses the right side and lower left, leaving upper-left 45% and the top 15% nearly plain for separate product text. Clear but restrained material motif, matching an established tyre card brand, not a poster or game scene. No text, no numbers, no logos, no actual tyre or wheel, no specification boxes, no border. Output full-bleed background only.
